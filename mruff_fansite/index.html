About Mark

Mark Ruffalo is an American actor, producer and director. He was born on November 22, 1967, in Kenosha, Wisconsin, and worked in Los Angeles theater for years before his breakout role in You Can Count on Me (2000).

He has earned four Academy Award nominations, for The Kids Are All Right, Foxcatcher, Spotlight and Poor Things, and won an Emmy for the HBO series I Know This Much Is True. Many fans know him best as Bruce Banner, the Hulk, in the Marvel movies.
